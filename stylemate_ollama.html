STYLEMATE FRONT-END MVP · Generated sample listings, prices and specifications. Not live Myntra/AJIO inventory. Product suitability and risk scores are simulated; verify retailer listing details before purchase. Retailer checkout, live stock and account actions are not connected. Sign-in and the wardrobe check are hardcoded front-end prototypes: the demo account (test / test) and its six backdated 2026 orders live in the page source, with no server, no real credentials and no order data. Search is wired to a local Ollama model: every query sends the whole catalogue plus your wardrobe to the model on localhost and renders the SKUs it returns, in the order it ranked them. If the model is unreachable the page falls back to keyword matching and says so above the results. Every request to the model is logged in the browser and downloadable as a dated JSON file from the Logs panel; nothing is sent anywhere except your own localhost.
